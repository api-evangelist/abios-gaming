# Abios

> Abios is the world leading B2B provider of esports data and statistics. Founded in early 2013 with its roots&nbsp;at the Stockholm School of Economics and Royal Institute of Technology, Abios now works with more than a 100&nbsp;clients from all over the world. The company started out as a calendar and statistics hub for esports where you could follow your favourite teams, players and tournaments. However, since 2016 the company has focused solely on providing and distributing such&nbsp;data to the large number of&nbsp;businesses entering esports. As the demand for more advanced data products grow in all sectors of the&nbsp;esports ecosystem Abios works closely with its clients to shape the future of esports.
